Although digital tools help teams solve problems quickly, successful communication still depends on listening carefully, asking clear questions, and showing empathy. When a customer reports a technical issue, a professional employee should remain calm, explain the next steps, and make sure the customer understands the solution.

Written Expression — Practice Tasks
